Welcome

I write dark dystopian action adventure and fantasy fiction, where survival comes at a cost and hope refuses to die.

My stories explore fractured worlds, inherited fear, and the quiet defiance of those who refuse to disappear. In worlds shaped by collapse and consequence, survival is never the end of the story; it is just the beginning.

I live in the western US where building fictional worlds is just one of my pastimes.
