It Came to Me in the Shower

The book that really got me started on my writing journey came to me in the shower, seriously. Maybe I was still in a dream state, but for whatever the reason, the whole story came to me along with walking, talking characters and the working title. The title proved to be a bit too clever to be the real title, but it inspired me. The story itself was so fun and fast moving that I got out of the shower as fast as I could and ran to a notebook, writing much of it still dripping wet.

Since then, I’ve had many ideas come to me as I was going to sleep, biking, walking, or just as I was waking up, and they always prove useful even if they don’t make it into a book. Makes me wonder what I could do if I could write while I was sleeping.

Usually, what comes to me is a character in action, and maybe some dialogue, plus the general feeling of the story and where it’s going. For instance, an image came to me of a girl alone in a cave with only her robot dog for company. I knew she had a journey to find her father and I knew her journey was complicated and filled with danger, only to realize that her father is a horrible person, a mad scientist of sorts who thinks only of himself. The heroine also meets some friends along the way who help her in her quest, and become her found family and that became the first book in my Shattered World Series.

Many of us tell ourselves stories and some of us write them down. Whatever way they come, ideas and stories are always welcome. I only wish there was more time to write. Speaking of writing, I better go and do that now.

Happy reading.

Julian
