Writing October 31, 2026

Hi, This Is Julian

I write dark dystopian action-adventure and fantasy fiction with many of the same or similar themes: found family, enemies to lovers or friends, the rebellious outsider, the reluctant heroine, lost magic/technology, good vs evil, and quests.

*****************

I love to create in many different ways, but creating with words is the most satisfying to me. I love writing something new, or continuing a current work in progress. I even love editing, which does sometimes slow me down.

Writing demands concentration as you're making up whole other worlds and the people and lives inside of them. When I’m writing, I feel that sense of creating something from nothing, which is the creator's dream. It gives you a sense of awe and fulfillment and eventually excitement at the possibility of sharing it with others.

Thus, I publish and share my work with you, dear reader.

If you have the desire to create, I wish you luck. Creating things is often a thankless path, but sharing your creative work at the end of that path is its own reward. If you enjoyed my books, please let me know.

Best wishes, Julian
