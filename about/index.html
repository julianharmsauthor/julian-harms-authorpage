About Julian Harms

I write dystopian sci-fi and fantasy, where survival comes at a cost and hope refuses to die.

My published novels, the Shattered World Series, are dystopian sci-fi, and I'm currently working on more fantasy novels. My stories explore fractured worlds, inherited fear, and the quiet defiance of those who refuse to disappear.

I live in the western US, where building fictional worlds is just one of my pastimes.
